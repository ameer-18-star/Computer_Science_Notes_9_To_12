   c) Evaluate what new risks and responsibilities appear as a business moves up the ladder.

### Topic 8.2.1 & 8.2.2: Problem-Solving & Adaptability

1. Explain the process of problem-solving and adaptability as presented in this unit.
   a) Describe Root Cause Analysis and SWOT Analysis as structured problem-solving tools, with an original example for each.
   b) Construct a full 5-Whys trace for a business scenario of your choice, showing all five "why" questions and the final root cause.
   c) Discuss how adaptability and resilience work together, using the Netflix and YouTube case studies as evidence.
   d) Design a complete pivot plan for a business facing a sudden, unexpected disruption of your choice.

2. Evaluate the claim that "a business's biggest advantage is not avoiding problems, but having a structured way to respond to them." Support your evaluation using at least two concepts from this unit (e.g., root cause analysis, SWOT, adaptability, resilience).

### Topic 8.3.1, 8.3.2 & 8.3.3: Ethics, Responsible Marketing, and Data Privacy

   a) Define ethical decision-making and explain the concept of a conflict of interest with an original example.

   c) Explain the two core rules of responsible marketing and construct one truthful and one misleading advertisement to illustrate the difference.
   d) Discuss the key requirements of Pakistan's Personal Data Protection Bill, GDPR, and COPPA, and evaluate why a Pakistani digital business might need to comply with all three simultaneously.

   b) Justify your choices using at least one law discussed in this unit.
